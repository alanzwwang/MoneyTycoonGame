💵 Buck Tycoon

Buy stuff → place it in its shop's yard → run through it to collect bucks!

  • W A S D / ← ↑ ↓ → — walk
  • E — open the shop you're standing next to
  • Click a shop — browse & buy (items go to your backpack)
  • Click a backpack item — carry it, then click a slot in that shop's yard to place it
  • Click a placed item — pick it back up (its stored bucks collect automatically)
  • Sell zone (red carpet, far left) — click it while carrying to sell for half price
  • Yellow bills float above items that have bucks to collect — run through them!
  • Esc — close / stash   M — mute   H — this help
