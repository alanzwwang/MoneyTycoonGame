A tycoon game where you build up shops and collect bucks to grow your empire.

## 🎮 How to play

- **Click a shop** to browse and buy. Items go into your backpack.
- **Click a backpack item** to carry it, then click a glowing slot in that shop's yard to place it.
- **Click a placed item** to pick it back up. The bucks it stored are collected automatically.
- Click the **SELL** zone to get half the price back.
- **Esc** closes a menu, or stashes what you're carrying back in your backpack.

## 🚀 Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install needed.
